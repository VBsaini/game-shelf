DECKHAND

Your deck is your crew. Put each sailor in the right place on the ship and sail through three seas of monsters.

Each turn you have 3 energy and draw 4 cards. Tap a card, then a spot on the deck.
Crew stay on deck and act every turn from stern to bow: Gunners fire properly only at the cannons, a Navigator at the helm gives +1 energy, Bosuns and Surgeons help the crew next to them.
The monster's next move is shown above it. Most attacks hit whoever stands at the bow first; cannon shots hit the hull. If the hull reaches 0, your ship sinks.
Keys: 1–8 pick a card, Q W E R pick a deck spot, Space ends the turn.
